These fictional comments were written to support the faculty exercise. They do not represent real students or course evaluations.

## Instructor Observation

Students often produce correct algebra after being told which op-amp rule to use. The harder outcomes are selecting the operating model, connecting equations to current paths, rejecting an inconsistent assumption, and explaining the circuit's purpose.
