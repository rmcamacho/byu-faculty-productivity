# Draft AI Policy for This Unit

> **DELIBERATELY INCOMPLETE TUTORIAL DRAFT — NOT A COURSE POLICY**

Students may use AI tools on op-amp homework as long as they use them responsibly. Students remain responsible for submitted work and should understand their answers. Inappropriate AI use may be considered an academic-integrity violation.

The instructor may ask a student to explain any submitted solution.

## Note to Reviewer

This draft has not yet defined permitted uses, prohibited uses, disclosure expectations, treatment of student or institutional data, or the evidence students must provide to demonstrate their own learning.
