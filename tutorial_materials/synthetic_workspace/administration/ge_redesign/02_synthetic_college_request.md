> **SYNTHETIC TUTORIAL DOCUMENT — NOT AN OFFICIAL BYU OR COLLEGE RECORD**

**To:** Department chairs and undergraduate coordinators
**From:** Fictional College Curriculum Committee
**Date:** August 22, 2026
**Subject:** Department input on the revised GE proposal

The college committee is preparing a consolidated response to the current General Education redesign materials. Please provide a concise department response addressing the following:

1. Overall support, opposition, or conditional support.
2. Anticipated effects on major requirements and time to graduation.
3. Faculty expertise that could contribute to UNIV courses or working groups.
4. Staffing, classroom, laboratory, and instructional-support needs.
5. Questions about quantitative reasoning, communication, AI literacy, or assessment.
6. Any issue that should be elevated to university leadership.

## Requested Format

- No more than 750 words.
- Separate confirmed facts from estimates.
- Identify issues requiring further analysis.
- Do not promise faculty participation or departmental resources without chair and dean approval.

## Deadline

Submit the department response to the fictional college committee by **12:00 noon Thursday, August 27, 2026**. The college plans to complete its response before the university's August 31 comment deadline.
