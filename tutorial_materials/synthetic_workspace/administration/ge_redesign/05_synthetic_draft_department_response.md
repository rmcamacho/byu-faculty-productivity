> **SYNTHETIC TUTORIAL DOCUMENT — NOT AN OFFICIAL BYU OR ECEN POSITION**
> **DRAFT — DO NOT SUBMIT**

The ECEN curriculum committee cannot support the revised General Education proposal until the university guarantees that ECEN 230 will replace UNIV 106 for all engineering students and provides permanent funding for four ECEN-taught UNIV sections each year.

The department supports the goals of a coherent, assessable, and distinctly BYU General Education program. We also support preparing students to use artificial intelligence. However, the current proposal does not explain what engineering students will learn in UNIV 106, how much duplication will occur, or which unit will be responsible for AI literacy.

ECEN is prepared to commit two faculty members to teach four sections annually beginning in fall 2027. We estimate that this will serve 160 students each year and require $24,000 in first-year support. The department expects all four sections to be credited to ECEN.

We request:

1. automatic substitution of ECEN 230 for UNIV 106;
2. a guarantee of permanent instructional funding;
3. authority for ECEN to determine the AI outcomes for UNIV 106;
4. two ECEN positions on each relevant course working group; and
5. an implementation delay until all engineering majors have completed curriculum revisions.

We appreciate the opportunity to comment and look forward to receiving answers before the proposal moves forward.
