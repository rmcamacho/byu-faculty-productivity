# Synthetic Resources and Constraints

> **SYNTHETIC TUTORIAL INFORMATION — NOT A BYU COMMITMENT OR FACILITIES STATEMENT**

## Available for the Exercise

- Standard circuit-simulation and PCB-design software.
- Benchtop power supplies, oscilloscopes, signal generators, and source-measure capability suitable for low-frequency sensor-interface prototypes.
- Two graduate-student research assistant roles and several undergraduate project roles are plausible planning assumptions.
- A three-year technical plan can be considered.
- Reproducible code, test fixtures, calibration datasets, and measurement procedures may be shared when appropriate.

## Not Yet Confirmed

- Access to a particular semiconductor process or fabrication run.
- A field site or external application partner.
- Specialized environmental chambers or long-duration deployment infrastructure.
- Preliminary data demonstrating adaptive calibration.
- Final personnel effort, student support, equipment purchases, indirect-cost treatment, or total budget.
- Any institutional commitment, cost sharing, subaward, consultant, or partner letter.

## Proposal Guardrails

Codex may propose questions and alternatives, but it must not convert an unconfirmed resource into a factual capability. A first concept should include a benchtop validation path that does not depend on custom fabrication or a field partner. Those elements can be optional later stages if the faculty member confirms them.
