# One-Page Proposal Concept Template

> **TUTORIAL PLANNING ARTIFACT — NOT A SPONSOR OR KUALI FORM**

## Working Title

Use a specific technical title, not a slogan.

## Faculty-Intended Direction

State how this concept advances the direction the faculty member approved.

## Problem and Foundational Gap

Describe the important engineering problem and the knowledge or method that does not yet exist. Separate the foundational gap from the application motivation.

## Central Research Question or Hypothesis

Write one falsifiable or otherwise assessable question.

## Proposed Approach

Summarize two or three coherent aims or work packages. Identify the mechanism, model, circuit, algorithm, experiment, or dataset each contributes.

## Validation and Go/No-Go Evidence

Name the benchmark, comparison baseline, measurement, and evidence that would support or challenge the central claim.

## Technical Risk and Fallback

Identify the most consequential risk and a fallback that still produces useful foundational knowledge.

## Student Roles and Broader Impacts

Identify substantive student contributions and a credible societal or educational benefit. Avoid generic participation claims.

## Sponsor and Program Fit

Connect the concept to exact program language and review criteria. State the strongest remaining fit question for a program officer.

## Assumptions Requiring Confirmation

List facilities, preliminary results, partners, personnel, budget, data, compliance, and institutional commitments that remain unresolved.
