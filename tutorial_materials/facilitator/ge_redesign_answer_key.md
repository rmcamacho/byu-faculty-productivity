### Department position

- Minutes: conditional support for continued development.
- Draft response: cannot support the proposal until guarantees are made.
- Impact analysis: recommends an immediate faculty commitment.
- Minutes: explicitly withhold commitments pending workload information.

### Major-course substitution

- Several synthetic files request ECEN 230 as a UNIV 106 substitute.
- The source brief says the current proposal does not allow major-specific substitutions.
- A responsible briefing should identify this as a request that conflicts with the current design, not describe it as an available option.

### Teaching capacity

- Impact analysis: two fall sections, one winter section, and one every other year, averaging 3.5—not four—sections annually.
- Claimed enrollment: 160 annually; the stated average and 40-seat assumption produce 140.

- Adding first-year faculty development produces $36,000.
- The analysis incorrectly calls $24,000 the first-year total.
- None of the numbers has a documented basis.

### Unsupported implementation claims

- The draft promises a fall 2027 start, but the official materials establish no implementation date.
- The draft commits two faculty members without approval.
- The draft demands permanent funding although the committee decided only to seek clarification.

### Missing or unclear ownership

- No owner is assigned to the AI-literacy recommendation.
- “Curriculum committee” is not a sufficiently specific owner for a time-sensitive capacity estimate.
- No one is assigned to reconcile the draft response with the official source brief.
- No one is assigned to verify the budget and enrollment calculations.

### Blocked task

The action to map ECEN outcomes against proposed course-level GE outcomes cannot be completed as written because those course-level outcomes do not yet exist. It should be reframed as mapping against the draft program outcomes or deferred until working groups publish course outcomes.

## Expected Recommendation

A strong response should:

- preserve conditional support for continued development;
- pose questions rather than demand guarantees not authorized by the committee;
- express interest in appropriate working-group participation without committing people or resources;
- ask how UNIV 106 will interface with engineering curricula and AI competencies;

- distinguish the department's interests from the current proposal's actual provisions.
